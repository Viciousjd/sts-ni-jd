01 / THE NANO WORLD

SMALL SCALE. BIG QUESTIONS.

What is nanotechnology?

Nanotechnology focuses on materials and technologies at an extremely small scale. Working with matter at this scale can create new properties and applications in areas such as medicine, electronics, materials, and environmental technologies.

But scientific progress does not happen separately from society. The development and use of new technologies can raise questions about safety, fairness, privacy, environmental responsibility, and regulation.
